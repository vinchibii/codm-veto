    How a veto runs

    1. 1
      Create a room

      Enter both teams, pick the format and adjust the map pool. You get a 6-character code and an invite link.

    2. 2
      Captains take their seats

      Each captain opens the link on their phone and claims their team. Everyone else can watch the same board.

    3. 3
      Bans, picks, then sides

      Bans come first, then every map is picked, then each map's starting side. Nothing is left over, and the sheet locks at the end.
